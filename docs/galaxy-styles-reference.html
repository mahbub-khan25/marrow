Galaxy map: which style feels right?

Each folder in the vault is a galaxy. Its notes are planets orbiting it. Subfolders are galaxies linked to their parent, and the whole vault is the Milky Way at the center. Dashed lines are connections you drew between galaxies. Hover a planet to see the note's name.

Sample vault: Physics (→ Quantum), Programming (→ Rust, Python), Study Notes, Journal. Your connections: Physics ↔ Programming, Study Notes ↔ Python.

A · Nebula

Rich and cinematic. Spiral galaxies made of glowing star-dust, soft nebula clouds behind them, luminous planets. Light mode is pearl-white with watercolor pastels.

B · Orbit

Clean and modern. Crisp orbit rings, solid colorful planets, bold labels and plenty of white space. Calm enough to keep open while you work.

C · Celestial Atlas

An antique star chart. Ink-drawn galaxies with compass ticks, notes joined like constellations, gold for your own connections. Cream paper in light mode, midnight navy in dark.
